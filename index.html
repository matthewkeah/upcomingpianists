Our vision

To be Kenya's foremost peer-led community for serious pianists — a space where excellence is built through honest critique, shared learning, and collective ambition, not solo grinding.

Our mission

A consistent, structured, and genuinely encouraging environment where pianists at every career stage can present their work, take real feedback, and grow toward public performance together.

What actually holds this together

Three foundations, chosen to fix the inconsistencies that sink most informal groups.

Structured sessions

Fixed to a permanent monthly anchor date. Ninety minutes of dedicated performance time, covering technical feedback, phrasing, and precise peer critique.

Distributed ownership

No single point of failure. Leadership rotates across Masterclass Coordinators, People's Coordinators, and Event Leads, each on a term with a renewal vote.

Artistic ambition

From isolated practice-room hours to confident public presence — building systematically toward showcases, café performances, and joint recitals.
