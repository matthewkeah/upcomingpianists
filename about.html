Organizational framework

Founded by pianists, for pianists. Formalizing a structure that honors our origins in Nairobi while equipping us for a sustainable future.

Our core values

Consistency

We show up, even when it's inconvenient. Predictability of schedule builds trust.

Honest critique

We give mutual peer feedback that is truthful, technically specific, and kind.

Shared ownership

Every active member is a stakeholder and driver, not a passive passenger.

Artistic ambition

We aim higher together toward public recitals than we would practicing alone.

Distributed leadership structure

To prevent burnout, roles are rotational and held for a maximum of one year without a renewal vote.

RoleCore responsibilitiesAccountability metric
Chairperson / ConvenerFacilitates live sessions, holds group vision.Elected annually by active members.
Masterclass CoordinatorSchedules sessions, confirms presenters, manages venue logistics.Sends monthly schedule at least 3 weeks in advance.
People's CoordinatorMember welfare, onboarding, tracks attendance trends.Flags disengagement early and conducts 1-on-1 outreach.
Secretary / ScribeDocuments session repertoire, records formal decisions.Circulates written notes within 48 hours.
